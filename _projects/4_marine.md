A collaboration between **NPL** and the **UK Hydrographic Office** to develop a standardised, machine-interpretable language for autonomous marine vessels — making significant strides toward a machine-readable model of the **S100 series** of navigational charts.

**Highlights**

- Developed an **LLM workflow** to translate tags from the S100 navigational charts into machine-readable format.
- Validated generated output as **XML against an XSD schema**.

*Techniques:* Large Language Models
*Key libraries:* Ollama
