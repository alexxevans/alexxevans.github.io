An extended literature review and self-study project at Imperial College London on the application of **diffusion models and novel samplers** to denoising medical images. The resulting paper was **awarded the highest grade in the cohort**.

**Highlights**

- Covered score-based approaches from **Hyvärinen score matching** to **Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models** and transformer architectures.
- Extended to novel **Tweedie-based samplers** for solving inverse problems and their potential application in medical imaging.

*Techniques:* Score Matching · Diffusion · Inverse Problems
