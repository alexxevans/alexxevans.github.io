At the **National Physical Laboratory (NPL)**, I developed a generative AI model for the European Space Agency **TRUTHS** mission (Traceable Radiometry Underpinning Terrestrial and Helio-Studies). The goal was to calibrate the satellite's onboard **Hyperspectral Imaging Spectrometer (HIS)**, retrieving solar irradiance from thermal readings to observe the effects of climate-change mitigation policy.

**Highlights**

- Modelled the encoded posterior using a **continuous-time diffusion model** with a transformer architecture for conditioning.
- Enabled samples of the measurand to be generated directly, providing reliable **uncertainty quantification** on predictions.

*Techniques:* Diffusion · Transformers · Uncertainty Quantification
*Key libraries:* JAX

This work was carried out within NPL's Earth Observation and Climate group.
